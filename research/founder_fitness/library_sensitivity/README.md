# Sensitivity to the measurement used

The day-0 leader beat the four-repeat combined founder forecast in all 128 endpoint-library assignments. It led by **1–11 correct calls across 76 wells**. With the authors’ selected readings, the scores were 45 and 40. With original-library endpoints throughout, they were 54 and 47. The combined projection also had higher mean absolute error in the final A share under every assignment, by 11.82–17.46 percentage points.

The check holds forecasts fixed at their published day-0 inputs and varies the day-25 reading for an entire sequencing pool at once. Seven pools, containing 70 wells, had paired readings at days 0, 10 and 25. Each of those seven pools can use its original or repeated endpoint. The eighth pool’s six wells stay at the source-selected original endpoint. Five of the six have a positive-depth repeat endpoint, one has zero reads, and all six lack baseline and day-10 reruns. These 128 assignments enumerate the complete-pool comparison. The assignments have no estimated probabilities. [Earlier release and normalized technical tables](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24382132) · [Source study](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.10118)

The ancestral-assay check holds the endpoint at the authors’ selected reading and uses a single assay repeat consistently within each of the 12 ancestral pools. Across the 4¹² possible assignments, the combined forecast ranges from 30 to 52 hits, against 45 for the day-0 leader. Its mean absolute share error exceeds the baseline by 6.44–26.01 percentage points. Each well belongs to one ancestral pool, so summing the per-pool minima and maxima gives the attainable bounds for each metric. Different metrics can reach their extrema under different choices. The four-repeat forecast remains the specified primary rule.

Run `python3 check_libraries.py` from this directory to regenerate `library_results.json` on standard output. The script verifies the hashes of the ancestor table, longitudinal counts, technical counts and endpoint pool memberships, then checks that the previous release’s selected technical counts equal the longitudinal counts at all three recorded visits.
