# Where the founder measurements come from

Fasanello, Liu, Botero and Fay (2020) retained samples of the undiluted starting mixtures from 76 paired populations of baker’s yeast. They later describe reviving ancestral and evolved samples and assaying the two generations in separate pools. Each founder's assigned pair was together in its ancestral pool, along with other labelled strains and a shared, unevolved reference of the same ploidy. Four 48-hour assay replicates measured changes in relative abundance over approximately 20 generations. The source material predates the long competition outcome. The publication does not document that assay results were available before that outcome. This supports retrospective testing with a separately measured ancestral predictor. [Paper, Methods and Fig. 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7571412/)

All assays used the medium corresponding to the long competition and a common standard transfer regime. Both media and transfer regime match for conditions 1–4 (54 wells). Conditions 5 and 6 used different transfer regimes during the long competition (22 wells). The ancestral assay compared several strains in one pool. The long competition followed two per well. Pool mates share a reference and sample-level measurement errors. The authors call the four measurements technical replicates. The same label and ploidy can also occur in more than one well. The source preparation code first separates pooled fitness-assay samples from longitudinal population samples. It arranges the four generation-zero repeats in four sets of columns. It then copies this same ancestral block onto four rows representing the evolved assay repeats. Collapsing the identical ancestral blocks leaves 152 founder-by-well records from 608 native rows. The extractor matches the native treatment and disambiguated founder IDs, checks both directions of every pair, and reads only metadata from the longitudinal table. Population IDs follow the earlier forecast release's W001–W076 row order. Within each well, A precedes B in lexical order of their original barcode IDs. This mapping also reproduces all 76 day-zero count orientations in the earlier release. The twelve F-prefixed pool IDs follow lexical order of the native fitness-assay set names. ## Reading the 304-row extraction

Each row describes one pair in one of the four ancestral assay replicates. `initial_A`, `final_A`, `initial_B` and `final_B` are the two focal read counts at the start and end of that assay. `initial_ref` and `final_ref` are the corresponding common reference counts. These fields retain the one pseudocount the authors added to each expected barcode count. `initial_pool_reads` and `final_pool_reads` retain their published totals over expected counts. Both the reference and the totals are shared across all rows in the same pool and replicate. These assay times differ from days 0 and 25 in the long competition. [Count preparation](https://github.com/VinceFasanello/MM_Code_Supplement/blob/a370be7497fda328c6c8b13d957223ff4cba53c6/2_CountstoAnalysis/002_Prepare_Datasets/002_Prepare_Datasets.Rmd#L72-L99)

`fit0_A` and `fit0_B` retain the authors’ per-generation relative fitness summaries, repeated unchanged across the four assay rows of each well. Within a replicate, they computed the focal-to-reference count ratio at assay end, divided by that ratio at assay start, and took the twentieth root. For each time point they formed the harmonic mean of focal, reference and pool read counts. The harmonic mean of the two time-point values provided the replicate weight. All the inputs to the weighted arithmetic mean come from the ancestral assay. The source R code uses `apply` on a mixed-type frame, which converts the columns to text before weighting. For these pinned data, rounding replicate fitnesses to seven decimal places and weights to four reproduces the original summaries within 5 × 10⁻¹⁶. Full-precision weights and fitnesses reproduce them within 3.4 × 10⁻⁸. The TSV preserves the native summary values. [Weight definitions](https://github.com/VinceFasanello/MM_Code_Supplement/blob/a370be7497fda328c6c8b13d957223ff4cba53c6/2_CountstoAnalysis/002_Prepare_Datasets/002_Prepare_Datasets.Rmd#L433-L456) · [Fitness calculation](https://github.com/VinceFasanello/MM_Code_Supplement/blob/a370be7497fda328c6c8b13d957223ff4cba53c6/2_CountstoAnalysis/002_Prepare_Datasets/002_Prepare_Datasets.Rmd#L495-L511)
