# R12 independent source and interpretation review

**Accepted:** the exact six core files pinned in `R12_REVIEW_RECEIPT.json`.
The reviewer performed no requests and inspected only cached public sources.
No private files, raw clinical measurements, author contact or publication
operation were used in this review.

The source scope is accurate: one targeted search returned 45 matching PubMed
records, eight returned candidates were screened from their metadata/abstracts,
two selected primary main-text methods/results were read, and NanoSpacer remains
an abstract-only lead. The narrative review is excluded as new primary evidence.
The four recorded response sizes sum to 506,673 bytes. All eight source-screen
identities, titles, journals, DOIs, PMC identifiers, available article dates and
inspection decisions agree with the cached batch XML. All four source snapshots
match their recorded sizes and hashes.

The main clinical paper is De La Motte et al., DOI
`10.1128/spectrum.03910-25`, PMC13228076. Its 72 within-method clinical pairs are
distinct from its 69/71 cross-method complete-case comparisons. Flow acquisition
events are not independently introduced individuals or tracked lineages. Its
reported 5,500/10 CFU median ratio is a ratio of marginal medians, not a paired
fold change, calibrated recovery probability or mutation-rate ratio. The P=.470
viability comparison does not establish equivalence or prove unchanged viability.
Membrane-integrity gating does not independently establish VBNC status.

The one-microlitre plating volume nominally gives 1,000 CFU per suspension mL
for one counted colony. A tenfold concentration adjustment would give 100 CFU
per original mL, if applicable. The stated <10 CFU/mL threshold and 10-CFU/mL
untreated median require an explicit volume, dilution, concentration and
censoring map. The candidate correctly presents this as an unresolved reporting
gap, rather than proof of a source error. Declared raw Data set S1 was not
acquired or reanalyzed; the calibration verifier does not claim otherwise.

The PA14 paper, DOI `10.1007/s00203-026-04995-3`, PMC13272224, uses redox/membrane
gates, an approximate OD-to-CFU mapping, variable flow-event acquisition and
post-antibiotic outgrowth. These are useful contrasts between operational
readouts and physiological states. They do not establish native SBW25 recovery,
absolute individual-cell introduction, a heritable mutation route, or mutation
births. Similar MIC profiles support the paper's persistence interpretation
without excluding every possible new mutation.

NanoSpacer, DOI `10.1039/d6lc00655h`, PMID42831674, has an electronic article
date of 5 October 2026 in the retrieved record. Its abstract's three fluorescent
mixture proportions are faithfully reported. Relative proportions do not
authenticate absolute capture or recovery. Its main text, replicate denominators
and supplements remain uninspected in this round.

The candidate's prospective calibration requirements preserve unit definition,
instrument enumeration, biological culturability, aggregation, missing or zero
terminal marks, lineage ancestry and native/control transport. It does not admit
a matched common-background Wsp/Aws/Mws panel, fill an actual registry field,
infer a biological mutation rate, validate TMD, or make a cancer-prevention claim.

Validation is reported separately: 70 producer checks replayed exactly; 39
independent frozen-primary DOI/license/text checks and four independent
NanoSpacer abstract anchors passed; 69 candidate scope/identity/replay/guard
checks passed. These overlap in subject matter and are not summed into a claim
of independent biological replication. Producer -O/-OO launches and a missing
source cache refuse verification without writing a result. Source hashes alone
are not restored source files.
