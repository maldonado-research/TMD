## Operational hypothesis

“Natural dominance” is a heritable route's propensity to produce a defined successful outcome in a specified genetic background and environment. This operational use differs from heterozygote dominance. Supply, accessibility, establishment, selection, drift and recovery can each generate unequal route outcomes. The restricted shared-curvature forecast must improve prediction in held-out contexts against measured competing explanations before it gains incremental support. One fitted context is saturated; a score certificate alone does not identify a mutation mechanism.
