# Questions for qualified biological and statistical review

**Uncommissioned design review; no actual inputs selected**

The requested outcome is a written feasibility/admissibility assessment with cited supporting records and explicit unresolved points. The public Brody pedigree lead, requirement matrix and synthetic ledger are starting materials, not a ready laboratory protocol or a validated cancer model.

| Decision to assess | Concrete answer requested | Evidence to provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matched background and ancestor | Can contexts share a verified founder/background while preserving relevant allele, copy-number and mosaic states? Which differences defeat matching? | Authentication, stock/founding records, ancestor/reference evidence, copy-state and context records. |
| Molecular catalogs and axis | What disjoint new-acquisition events are scientifically meaningful? What justifies an ordered three-route axis before selected outcomes? | Catalog/reference version, mechanistic rationale, opportunity definitions, episode/multi-hit/Other rules. “No justified triad” is an acceptable answer. |
| Pedigree exposure | Which divisions and time intervals are directly observed, and where are identity/coverage gaps? | Example annotated pedigree, transfer/identity receipts, exposure/callability definitions and uncertainty. |
| Lost genotypes | Which dead, lost, unrecovered or failed-outgrowth lineages can be genotyped or bounded? What remains unknowable? | Fate-specific observation records, independent validation and defensible missingness limits; no imputed negatives. |
| Event inclusion predicate | What exact native rule includes an origin, and what evidence separates shared inheritance from leaf, recurrence or post-expansion ambiguity? | Caller/version/threshold predicate, reference/outgroup requirements, origin adjudication examples and known truth/orthogonal checks. |
| Native controls and transport | Which cell-stage and origin-event controls are feasible and sufficiently state/route matched? How is transfer failure measured or bounded? | Independent known-input denominators, successes/failures, carrier structure, assay validation, batch and transport evidence. |
| Biological replication and dependence | What is one independent unit? Which observations are nested, paired or linked by shared founder/batch? | Sampling/allocation and dependence graph, bottleneck/randomization/blinding rationale, full denominator ledger. |
| Inference, error and stopping | Which observation law and uncertainty procedure are admitted for those units? What fixes thresholds, stopping/looks, multiplicity and held-out predictions? | Prespecified analysis/design document, simulations under meaningful alternatives, independent eta rule, practical effect/score criteria and reviewable amendments. |

Separate a feasible measurement pilot from a TMD test: obtaining callable descendants alone does not establish a matched independent supply/recovery panel or validate a restriction. Compare mutation production, growth/loss, genotype state, recovery and sampling explanations with equal access to eligible data.

Requested answers should identify which claim each piece of evidence supports and what it cannot establish. No contact, partner commitment, price, laboratory scope, study registration, prevention endpoint or intervention is supplied or commissioned by this packet.
