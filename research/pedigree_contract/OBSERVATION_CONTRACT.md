# Prospective pedigree and event-observation contract

**Candidate requirements; actual study choices unresolved**

The following defines an observation architecture to review, not a laboratory protocol or a commissioned study. The synthetic ledger illustrates fields without supplying actual biological choices. A qualified design must first establish its system, route/event units, observation rules and usable controls.

## Identity, ancestry and exposure

Retain an immutable culture/founder identity with background, stock history, donor or clone, batch, occasion and context. Authentication, founding genotype, allelic/copy-number state, pre-existing mosaicism and reference uncertainty must be recorded. Different backgrounds cannot silently become matched conditions. Keep the dependence graph: descendants of one founder, repeated images, genomes and paired contexts remain related observations.

For each tracked cell segment, retain parent identity, daughter links, observed entry and exit intervals, division records, observation-clock units, imaging coverage and reasons tracking ends. Document release/reseeding and identity uncertainty. A sequence-inferred edge must be labeled inferred and cannot replace a directly observed division count.

Distinguish observed divisions, tracked cell-time and callable genomic opportunity. None is automatically the correct mutation-supply denominator. Division counts must not be inferred by dividing variant counts by an assumed mutation rate. An observed division does not itself prove independent molecular trials or exact mutation time.

## Genotype observations, origins and inheritance

Store each assayed target call with sample/cell lineage, immutable reference and callable territory, allele and copy state, raw-to-call receipt, thresholds, validation status and explicit alternate/reference/unknown outcome. Missing rows are not negative calls. Count physical assays and sequencing reads separately from cells and hypothesized origins.

Keep one origin-hypothesis record per eligible molecular event or episode, linked to all supporting inherited observations. Preserve candidate lineage segments and time windows, alternative origin histories, outgroup/reference support, pre-existing-origin possibility, recurrence/allelic loss and call uncertainty. Multiple neighboring SNVs can share one damage episode; the actual event unit needs a fixed rationale. A shared allele in descendants is evidence of inheritance, not repeated acquisition.

Leaf-only observations, post-expansion variants, technically unresolved calls, Other events and uncertain multi-hit/episode states remain distinct. A branch interval is not an exact first-successful-arrival time. No event may enter a generic triad merely because it is cancer-associated.

## Loss and ascertainment ledgers

Maintain every observed terminal fate: alive at collection, death, tracking loss, release not recovered, isolation failure, outgrowth failure, sequencing omission/failure and assay no-call. Record both stage-specific eligibility denominators and the universe they exclude. A survivor sample cannot reconstruct the genotype of an extinct branch by declaration.

Use separate control ledgers for:

1. **Cell-stage recovery:** known eligible units and observed transfer/isolation/viability/outgrowth/sequencing outcomes, with batch and state matching.
2. **Event inclusion:** a scientifically justified known-origin truth set or orthogonal evidence, its carrier structure and required reference/outgroup states, and the complete predicate for an origin to enter the analyzed event ledger.

These are different targets. Good cell recovery does not guarantee every origin survives with enough callable descendants; good caller consistency does not calibrate genotype-specific loss. Native state/route transport and uncertainty must be independently measured or bounded. Same-outcome fitting, consensus agreement and nominal device geometry do not supply independent truth.

## Immutable design and inference gates

Freeze actual reference/catalog version, disjoint route definitions, episode handling, axis rationale, inclusion predicate, quality and fate thresholds, excluded states and observation horizon before selected test outcomes. Preserve signed or independently witnessed version/time receipts and all amendments. A hash alone establishes bytes rather than timing or scientific validity.

Lock founder sampling, biological replicate/dependence unit, selected-outcome rule, censoring/missingness treatment, stopping/looks, allocation, joint uncertainty, multiplicity/error budget, comparison models and held-out prediction rules. A complete forecast needs independently fixed held-out eta as well as curvature. Actual values and approved statistical likelihood remain unresolved here.

The original source's Poisson, binomial or caller-scoring conventions are not automatically valid for a new system. Retrospective marker reconstruction, expected endpoint mass, branch-acquisition records and one-outcome-per-culture events are different observation laws. A pilot requires qualified biological/statistical review and appropriate data/privacy permissions. This document authorizes no contact, commissioning, intervention or cancer-prevention conclusion.
