## The discriminating question and its limits

The existing [prospective candidate](../prospective_design/README.md) forecasts observed conditional-triad outcomes with a fixed axis, independently measured offsets, a common curvature parameter theta and a separately predicted context tilt eta. One positive triad is saturated. A common theta across contexts imposes restrictions, but common curvature alone neither supplies a complete new-context forecast nor identifies a mutation-generation mechanism. Conditional elimination of eta is a weaker contrast test. It does not replace freezing eta before held-out outcomes.

The practical question is whether independent supply, lineage establishment, ecological competition and observation measurements predict the **same complete W/A/M probability vector** as well as or better than the restricted forecast, on the same held-out independent runs. The primary estimand remains the existing conditional-triad score. Other, no-qualified-outcome, unresolved classification and missing outcomes must remain visible with their denominators. An all-category forecast or an origin-rate target would require a separately admitted and frozen observation model; this contract does not silently change the primary score.

There are two distinct assessment branches. Observed prediction requires an authenticated endpoint classifier, independent eligible trials and frozen probabilities. A biological interpretation additionally requires supply-event definitions, origin truth, context-matched control transfer, and loss limits. A good observed forecast does not certify that mutation origins were identified. Conversely, an inability to identify origins does not logically prohibit a correctly scoped observed prediction if its own inputs and count law were independently admitted. Neither branch is ready under the inspected material.

## A control ladder a qualified team can assess

The following defines what a protocol must measure, not actual strains, laboratory conditions, timings, sample sizes or a selected partner. Each control has an explicit unit, denominator and failure interpretation in the machine-readable contract.

1. **Authenticate the background and the outcome registry.** Establish source stock, parental genotype, eligible allele-to-route mapping, marker identity and an independent molecular confirmation method. Keep compound, Other and ambiguous events. Record the founder/run/batch hierarchy and the biological cluster to be held together in any split. Smooth morphology or an ancestral colour cannot certify absence of an eligible starting variant.
2. **Measure supply independently of endpoint winners.** Define acquisition episodes and mutation-opportunity exposure in the common genetic background and relevant contexts, with pre-existing/inherited truth and an explicit missing/lost-origin limit. Preserve the event ledger apart from descendant counts. Use a justified fluctuation or lineage likelihood when the experiment produces jackpots; a plate histogram is not automatically an independent binomial event count. A source's historical combined WS rate is not a route-resolved target-context supply calibration.
3. **Challenge establishment with known introduced eligible lineages.** For each route/allele and context, record introduction identity and the independently verified number of lineage trials, then ascertain a predefined establishment event at a fixed stage before the separate terminal recovery process. Preserve true loss, failed introduction, unobserved establishment and later recovery failure as different outcomes. Establishment must be measured by a separately calibrated observer/reference, or bounded with that observer's error; defining establishment as “a final colony was recovered” cannot separate the two stages. If multiple focal lineages share a well, their joint fate is clustered and a simple independent binary denominator needs separate justification.
4. **Separate ecological context from genotype.** Across controls, document resident ancestral state, resident abundance, focal inoculum/lineage multiplicity, introduction or emergence time relative to resident development, spatial/mixing state, medium, ΔpvdS status, plasmid and integrated-marker background. Where biologically justified, a qualified team can use blocked allocation to resident-present/absent or altered resident-state conditions, with density and timing deliberately matched or independently varied. Actual factor levels and allocation remain unchosen. A simultaneous mixture and a lineage appearing later within an existing scaffold are different treatments. A deletion background may also alter motility, growth or matrix production; it is a mechanistic probe requiring its own controls, not automatically the same background or a selective intervention on one ecological mediator. Marker swaps or marker-free confirmation must assess tag effects without redefining routes by colour.
5. **Calibrate recovery and classification separately in both phases.** Challenge known route/allele truths in the relevant physiological and spatial states at a declared assay entry stage. Retain the denominator introduced at that stage and every correct, incorrect, ambiguous and unrecovered result. Technical aliquots share a founder/control unit. A late spike-in calibrates only the steps after that entry point; it cannot establish earlier detachment, extraction, death or lineage loss. If off-diagonal route misclassification is possible, a full truth-by-called-category observation map is needed for a biological full-vector claim. Correct-recovery probabilities alone are not a confusion matrix. Transport from introduced controls to native lineages needs independent justification and uncertainty, by route × context × baseline/selected phase.
6. **Audit origin labels with independent positive and negative truth.** Independently established newly acquired episodes are origin-positive controls. Genotype-confirmed inherited/pre-existing variants are variant-positive but origin-negative controls; they test false de novo labels. Truly absent variants test variant false positives instead. Molecular identity alone cannot distinguish recurrence, inheritance or acquisition by transfer. Unobserved or destroyed lineages have unknown origin status unless an admitted independent method establishes it. Blinded adjudication must preserve missing truth, and control selection cannot condition solely on the assay's successful calls.
7. **Freeze forecasts, then evaluate independent endpoint runs.** Use independent calibration data and training contexts to freeze every target-context input, admissibility rule, complete comparator vector and existing TMD eta rule before unblinding held-out endpoint results. Keep paired arms, shared founders, dates/batches and descendants in their declared clusters. Assess forecast performance with the existing count-law, uncertainty, error-allocation and fixed-stopping gates. No chosen values or new error budgets are supplied by this contract.

Known introductions establish a control's own denominator only. They do not reveal how many unseen native lineages arose. Even error-free recovery of a control at one stage does not remove missing earlier stages. This is why source genotypes and a small positive mat comparison can motivate the programme without admitting it.

## What would discriminate the explanations

| Independent evidence and frozen evaluation | Permitted conclusion | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resident/density/timing manipulation changes independently adjudicated lineage establishment under a valid allocated design | An ecological contribution to that operational establishment outcome in the tested conditions | No automatic inference about mutation birth, full native transport or the complete endpoint vector |
| Independently measured supply differs by route/context | A supply explanation is available under its admitted event and observation model | Recovered descendants and selected isolates alone cannot provide it |
| Stage-specific recovery or classification differs by route/phase | An observation explanation is available for the affected stage | Earlier invisible loss and control-to-native transport remain separate |
| Independently parameterized conventional forecast matches or outperforms the restricted forecast under the frozen score gates | No demonstrated incremental predictive support for the restricted forecast in the evaluated frame | This need not identify a unique ecological mechanism |
| Restricted forecast improves on all frozen admitted comparators under the declared gates | Incremental observed predictive performance in the evaluated frame | Not discovery of a new mutation force or exclusion of unmeasured conventional mechanisms |
| Valid simultaneous biological contrast regions have empty common-theta intersection | Incompatibility with the common-curvature restriction under the admitted inputs and transfer assumptions | Not universal biological falsification outside that frame |
| Regions overlap, controls are unbounded, truth is missing, or score gates are unresolved | Compatible or unevaluable as appropriate | No positive support from mere non-rejection |

The conventional forecast must include the relevant supply, demographic, spatial/facilitation, competition and observation inputs under a justified generative model. Simply multiplying normalized supply by a marginal establishment probability can fail for competing multiple origins, frequency dependence or time-varying residents. The existing `normalize(qstar × F)` remains its declared candidate comparator, not a universal population-genetic identity. A richer conventional comparator can be developed using training/design information, but its exact implementation and complete vector must be frozen as a separately documented candidate before held-out outcomes; this addendum does not alter the current algorithm.

For a limited diagnostic only, if a justified positive route-weight model **does** factor as weights proportional to q × F × r, its supply-adjusted log curvature equals the establishment log curvature plus the recovery log curvature. This is the standard log-product identity already underlying [R18](../curvature_bounds/README.md). It explains what independent controls could account for; it is not a new estimator or proof that a race endpoint follows that factorization. Normalized expected abundance, a lineage-fate probability and a categorical run winner must not be substituted for one another. Even corrected curvature equal to one is insufficient for a full conventional-vector fit when an unrestricted axis tilt remains.
