# Mutation records in peach leaves and grapevines, version 0.9.1

Published 19 November 2026 Pacific in the Mutation by Natural Dominance report and methods series. The minimum number of edits needed to make the peach leaf catalogue compatible with one lineage per leaf is nine when additions and removals have equal cost. Allowing only additions raises the minimum to twelve. Holding the calls fixed and allowing mixtures requires at least three lineage signatures in each of five leaves. These are assumptions about the records and cell lineages. The biological prediction about which route first produces a successful heritable outcome remains open. [Zenodo 0.9.1](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24378187) · [Report series](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23068055) · [Research report](../leaf_models/README.md)

A separate native workbook covers 185 grapevines. After reconciling discrepancies at three sites, both source presentations show that 135 additional vines distinguish 12 of 1,378 marker pairs on the 53 concordant sites. [Workbook comparison](../leaf_models/grape/GRAPES.md)
