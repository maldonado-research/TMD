# A direct check against every leaf drawn in Figure 4

Wang et al. printed the numbers of the detected mutations beside individual leaf icons in their DHQ1 tree diagram. Two independent transcriptions agreed on the marker lists drawn at all 46 labelled locations. Together the icons display 148 memberships for 48 markers in 75 leaves. We transcribed the leaf labels directly, expanding printed numeric ranges into their individual markers. Numbers attached to the lightning symbols locate the authors’ proposed origins and were excluded from this transcription. The native publisher PDF is 2,808,349 bytes with SHA-256 `29d6df56c39e34c01099cbf64fb62a19a5d57b6b60e28b2cb1a04c8f75218aeb`. Figure 4 occupies page 14. [Publisher PDF](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3000191&type=printable) · [Figure 4](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000191.g004) · [Transcribed leaf annotations](figure4_leaf_icons.tsv)

The diagram labels positions on shoots and assigns numbers to mutations. The source tables identify leaves by sample name and sites by genomic coordinate. To compare the two, we retained each leaf’s position and allowed the icons to occur in either order at a two-leaf location. For each mutation we computed its list of carrier locations with multiplicity. A leaf and an icon can match only when they carry the same multiset of such lists. We then considered the remaining location-respecting assignments, requiring the multiset of marker columns to agree at each additional group of assigned leaves. A full assignment provided a single consistent row and column mapping. Checking each entry under that mapping reproduced **all 3,600 entries** in the catalogue membership matrix. Eight locations admitted more than one candidate icon ordering at the preliminary comparison. Several marker columns also have identical memberships. The verification establishes agreement up to these labels. [Verifier](figure_isomorphism.py) · [Original sample table](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000191.s018) · [Original mutation catalogue](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000191.s019)

Counting directly from the diagram yielded 1,055 disjoint, 54 strictly nested, nine identical and ten crossing pairs. The ten crossings in the diagram’s mutation labels are (1,2), (1,3), (2,3), (2,4), (3,4), (6,7), (9,11), (9,12), (10,11) and (10,12). Because the matrices match entry by entry up to labels, their optimal counts of unweighted row deletions, column deletions and single-entry changes needed to eliminate all crossings also agree. The diagram and catalogue are two presentations of the same set of 2016 leaf observations. Their agreement checks transcription and calculation. It supplies no independent biological replication or same-cell ancestry. [Exact receipt](figure_match.json)
