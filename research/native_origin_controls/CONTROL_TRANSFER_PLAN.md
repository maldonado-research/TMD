# Native control and transfer evidence plan

**Prospective expert-review requirements; actual inputs unresolved**

| Evidence level | Useful independent denominator | What it can support | What remains outside its claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instrument and library | Known input material, empty controls, callable territory and batch outcomes | Technical contamination, coverage and declared library performance | Eligible cells, unseen genotypes, true origin time or biological retention |
| Genotype/variant calls | Independently characterized positives and negatives with locus/copy/allele state | True/false call performance for the specified state and full frozen algorithm | New origin rather than inherited/pre-existing variant; extinct cells |
| Pedigree event calls | Independently justified distinct-event truth, carriers, reference states and adjudication | Inclusion of the specified origin unit through the complete caller | Origins missing from the truth universe or transport to unmatched routes/states |
| Native biological transfer | Controls matched to founder, context, state, route and survival/assay pipeline, with independent failures | Explicitly supported transfer or prespecified bounded mismatch | A universal capture parameter or a validated mutation mechanism |

An engineered allele, barcode, synthetic DNA spike-in or high-depth bulk reference may validate a specified subproblem. It does not automatically reproduce naturally arising mutation origins, copy/episode states, lethal loss or the entire native event pipeline. State exactly which stage starts after the intervention. Avoid labeling unknown-origin truth as known merely because a variant was introduced.

For each candidate control, demand independent truth provenance, target universe and the full ledger of eligible units, failures, positive/negative/unknown outcomes, carrier structure, reference support and batch/dependence. A known genotype-negative truth set checks false-positive variant calls within its declared frame; false event-origin calls additionally require independently justified ancestry/episode truth, including inherited or pre-existing variant states. Positive-call sensitivity alone cannot establish either complete error target. Multiple known events sharing one founder/cell are not independent Bernoulli control trials. Any binomial or other law and simultaneous uncertainty must be justified for the actual replication unit.

Freeze the reference, calling/recurrence/quality rules and sample-adaptation algorithm before test outcomes. A prespecified algorithm may adapt within a sample, but validation must evaluate that complete procedure on untouched appropriate truth; a tuned threshold or reconstructed spectrum is not itself independent truth. Record truth-set ascertainment, loss, copy-number, allele balance, locus class, background, burden and state mismatch. Predefine which mismatches invalidate transfer and which independently justified bounds enter uncertainty. No numeric mismatch or new statistical error allocation is chosen here.

Before any TMD inference, qualified reviewers must establish the event catalog and ordered axis, common background, independent molecular opportunity/exposure, growth/loss and unknown-origin limits, native inclusion controls, admitted count/dependence law, held-out parameters, fixed stopping and fair competing models. Calibration failure, insufficient origin truth or zero-inclusion route makes the proposed interpretation unevaluable. A successful calibration only enables an eligible test; it does not confirm TMD.

Useful alternatives include ordinary mutation/repair/exposure differences; selection and founder loss; inheritance/allele loss; reference or spectrum filtering; recurrent artifacts; and state-dependent assay recovery. A cancer-relevant mutagenicity pilot can be a measurement feasibility study without being a prevention experiment or a test of TMD's fixed-axis restriction.
