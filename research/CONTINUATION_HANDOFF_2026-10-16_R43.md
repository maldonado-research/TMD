Ricardo Maldonado · 16 October 2026 · Original analysis and software with AI assistance

The [source report](barcode_identity_audit/REPORT.md), [ten-claim audit](barcode_identity_audit/AUDIT.json) and [provenance](barcode_identity_audit/SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json) extend the R42 evidence upstream to original-author barcode extraction and combining scripts. Eleven source identities and fourteen exact anchors were checked against new and cached artifacts. The extraction/combining recipes and six nominal time labels do not authenticate actual execution, clock calibration, retained rows or genotype truth. No individual barcode-to-CNV assignments or missing CSV inputs were recovered.

The observation contract separates extraction, retention, aggregation, numerical export, ordered identifier binding, CNV annotation and display. Matching lengths cannot authenticate a positional join. A missing joined annotation does not establish a negative genotype. Source-code possibilities such as duplicates, padding, aggregation or misalignment have not been demonstrated in the actual inputs. The cached notebook's two saved raw shapes and its 1,772 likelihood-selected rows are not synchronized with the R42 1,569 positive-fitness grouped rows. Different predicates and unlinked saved views do not establish attrition, a subset, lost lineages or author error. No quality-threshold error is claimed from the literal script tokens.

The original [preflight utility](barcode_identity_audit/preflight.py), [contract](barcode_identity_audit/VALIDATION_CONTRACT.json) and [tests](barcode_identity_audit/test_preflight.py) make the structural requirements executable on analyst-prepared JSON. Explicit experiment/population/barcode keys and row roles are mandatory. Duplicate result/annotation identities and positional-only arrays are rejected; zero estimates are retained; unmatched annotations stay unknown; explicit aggregate/padding rows and orphan annotations remain accounted. There is no automatic population alias or identifier normalization.

Eight adversarial tests pass and a separate internal checker verifies 67 additional assertions. These are original synthetic table examples, not source rows or a biological simulation. Two different supplied bindings can both pass structurally, showing why external identity/role provenance remains necessary. All biological, source-binding, genotype-negative, clock, execution and native-admission flags remain false on structural PASS. The utility is a narrow normalized format, not a complete assay-state schema, raw-file converter, source replay or frozen TMD admission rule. Original input records and their full assay states must be preserved separately.

Five scientific requests saved 84,306 bytes across ten capture/receipt files. No new source-body extract copies were saved. Seven permitted requests remain unused. One native-system current-source search returned a primary source already assessed, so it was not reacquired. No exhaustive-web or global-absence claim follows. Four new small source records were acquired; the unrelated rendered Figure 6 notebook was not acquired.
