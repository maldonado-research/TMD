# Independent review of the R000009 input-authentication audit

Accepted as an original, bounded provenance audit of the published Farr et al. reporter assay. This review read all eight package core files, the source Figure 4A worksheet and the final main-article rate, interval, unit and method anchors. It independently reconstructed 24 culture occurrences and 16 ordered quantity vectors from the previously acquired SHA-pinned ZIP. Eighty exact rational digest, range, integrality and vector-length comparisons passed; two checks matched the article's reported rate/interval/unit anchors. No additional source acquisition or FALCOR execution occurred.

Nine of twelve WT confirmation-corrected candidate-pool estimates H L/M are noninteger; all twelve deletion-background estimates are integer. These are reconstructed estimates, not authenticated historical submissions or mutation-birth observations. Counts, density estimates per mL, nominal six-mL total CFU and per-replication rate outputs retain distinct units. Exact-rational vector hashes use the declared JSON serialization, not Excel cached decimal bytes. A future run export must reconcile rounding, settings, population and partial-plating conventions before comparison. Hashes alone establish neither reproduction nor error in the original estimator.

The present Figure 4A data provide pooled plate counts and nested colony confirmation rather than labelled paired-aliquot genotype counts. They therefore cannot fit the proposed paired-count observation law. All 24 Figure 4A confirmed counts are positive; the separate destructive time-course initial confirmation zero is a different sampling frame. Transformant and occasion labels are retained. Historical FALCOR input, server build, settings, interval recipe and author-response bodies remain unauthenticated. The four GET outcomes (50,304 recorded bytes) establish this environment's limited access, not absence of the underlying software or historical artifacts. Six OOXML and five literal RTF searches do not establish general artifact completeness; two binary XLS semantic parses remain UNRUN.

The producer source verifier and independent oracle reject optimized Python launch before verification, avoiding a false PASS when assertions are disabled. Both negative launches were tested. The normal independent reconstruction passes, and the producer reports a byte-identical normal replay after its guard-only update. This is independent internal review rather than external peer review, a new biological fit, a confidence interval reproduction or mathematical priority claim. All 26 actual TMD inputs remain NULL; previous score laws and error budgets are unaffected.
