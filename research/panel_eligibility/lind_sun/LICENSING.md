# Attribution and publication scope

Audit date: 2026-10-02 UTC.

The notes, eligibility decisions, missing-measurement map, manifests and reproduction code are original audit material. This subtask does not assign a new license to those originals; the enclosing project's publication terms govern them. Public-safe status is a content/provenance assessment, not an invented license grant.

Primary sources:

- Lind, Libby, Herzog and Rainey (2019), *Predicting mutational routes to new adaptive phenotypes*, DOI 10.7554/eLife.38822, PMC6324874. Main-article permissions explicitly state Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
- Sun and Lind (2023), *Distribution of mutation rates challenges evolutionary predictability*, DOI 10.1099/mic.0.001323, PMC10268835. Main-article permissions explicitly link Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

The short evidence excerpts retain source DOI, source hash and XML locator. `sun_aws_table3_transcription.csv` is an attributed factual secondary transcription of Sun/Lind Appendix B Table 3, whose experimental observations explicitly reuse Lind 2019. It is not a new collection.

Third-party raw XML, response metadata and source cache files are excluded from publication by `PUBLIC_FILES.json` and ignored `raw/`. The source mutation/fitness XLSX files were not acquired; their contents and any file-specific notices remain unverified. No private archive material, credential or unpublished experimental count is included. No large sequence/archive download was made.
