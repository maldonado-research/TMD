# PTATO artifact-admission worksheet

Mutation by Natural Dominance · R000023 · 8 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles

This original worksheet recovers the unpublished planning checkpoint. The candidate is PTATO 2023 paired clonal bulk-WGS/PTA validation material, DOI [10.1016/j.xgen.2023.100389](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xgen.2023.100389). Its starting decision was **DESCRIPTION_ONLY**, based on R21's limited selected-paragraph assessment. That starting checkpoint contained no new artifact inspection or admitted dataset. Subsequent inspection is recorded separately in this package.

The following are admission questions, not a new statistical rejection threshold. An official link alone does not pass a gate. Unknown labels and uninspected components must remain explicit.

| Gate | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Artifact identity | Official accession, release/version, experiment-to-release mapping, complete file manifest and roles; sizes/hashes for acquired bytes; missing components. |
| 2. Access and reuse | Artifact-specific access, reuse and privacy conditions. Article licensing does not establish every data artifact's conditions. |
| 3. Truth labels | Positive, negative and unknown labels; independent label generation/selection; separation of training from untouched evaluation. |
| 4. Complete caller | Reference/software versions, quality and callability rules, recurrence filters and sample adaptation. |
| 5. Denominators | Eligible variants/loci/cells, failures, exclusions, uncallable observations, unknown truth and each metric's denominator. |
| 6. Biological units | Founder, clone/culture, donor/animal and batch relationships; technical, nested and paired observations distinguished from independent units. |
| 7. Origin/episode truth | Independently established ancestry, standing/pre-existing variants and acquisition episodes for new-origin assignment. |
| 8. Missing lineages | Lost/unrecovered lineages and limits on origin-catalogue completeness. |
| 9. Native transport | Justified matching or transport to the intended native system and observation frame. |

Variant truth supports a narrowly conditioned genotype/allele benchmark. Origin truth additionally establishes whether acquisition is new and which episode it belongs to. Observed divisions support exposure within the monitored window; culture duration supplies neither division counts nor the genotypes of lost cells.

Caller-selected or estimated labels permit only an accurately named conditional or proxy comparison. Variant-stage admission does not automatically admit native origin inclusion, mutation production per division, qualifying-route count laws, a TMD fit or cancer-prevention conclusions.
