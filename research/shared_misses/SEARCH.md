# Which plant studies can check a shared negative?

On 19 November 2026 (Pacific), we screened current plant primary papers for replicate measurements or independent adjudication of mutation calls. The question was whether a study counted true mutations among sites classified as reference in both measurements. Firecrawl research-index searches combined the terms below. We read the primary articles for the comparison unit, how records entered validation and which denominator was available.

- `2026 plant somatic mutation replicate sequencing technical validation split samples sweet orange single tree`
- `2026 plant somatic mutation lineage tree Arabidopsis replicated technical sequencing validation fruit tissue mutation detection sequencing false negatives`
- `2026 meristem single cell somatic mutations validation replicated sequencing somatic mutation plants`

**Sweet orange, repeated libraries from the same DNA.** In six of the 28 original samples from one navel orange tree, two additional libraries used the same DNA source. Among original leaf-specific calls, 49–69% were recovered in at least one of the two additional libraries, depending on the comparison. The authors report 100% recovery under that combined rule for the clonal and mosaic comparisons. This result measures repeat recovery among original positives. A locus missed from the outset cannot enter that denominator. [Primary paper, “Additional sequencing of technical replicates”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13690466/) · [Supplement, Tables S3–S5](https://pmc-oa-opendata.s3.amazonaws.com/PMC13690466.1/koag288_supplementary_data.pdf)

**Broadleaf cattail, independent confirmation of selected genotypes.** Zhao et al. report confirmation by Sanger sequencing of 44 heterozygous SNPs in 11 selected regions across 72 sampled plants. The shared variants support the interpretation that the plants belonged to a single clone. The loci were chosen for validation. The study does not give the fraction of genome-wide double-reference sites independently examined and found to carry a mutation. [Primary paper, Figure 3 and Supplementary Figures 2–3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-026-10362-1)

**Arabidopsis, replicate assemblies.** The authors compared three assemblies for each of two plants and examined underlying alignments at differences. They identified 266–577 errors per PacBio assembly and 10,801–10,974 per Oxford Nanopore assembly. Highly repetitive nucleolus organizer regions remained unresolved. These counts show how many assembly differences the review attributed to error on that frame. They supply no per-site sensitivity for a low-frequency mutation missed in both halves of a tissue sample. [Primary paper, Figure 1 and Extended Data Figure 1](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11046-w)

These studies measure recovery of a previously reported call, independent confirmation of selected genotypes and adjudication of assembly differences. Each comparison has its own selection rule. None supplies a transferable truth fraction for the peach double-reference cells or a prospective test of the first route to a successful heritable outcome. The scope was a targeted primary-source screen.
