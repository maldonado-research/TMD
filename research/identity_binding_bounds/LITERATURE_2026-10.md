# Recent research screen: context for TMD

Ricardo Maldonado · 27 October 2026 · Research screen with AI assistance. This is a bounded, abstract-level screen, not a full-text or dataset audit. It does not establish a native matched route/control panel, mutation-origin rate, or validation of TMD.

- Al-Tameemi, Rosazza and Rodríguez-Verdugo, [Unilateral cross-feeding constrains adaptive evolution](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42904594/), online 21 October 2026, report 800 generations of experimental evolution involving *Acinetobacter johnsonii* and *Pseudomonas putida*. Their abstract reports differences between co-culture and monoculture in sequence-based measures and lineage patterns. This raises ecological context as a possible influence on observed evolutionary outcomes; πN/πS is not itself a mutation-rate estimate, and the abstract does not establish a decomposition of mutation supply, establishment, and recovery.
- Large et al., [Mutational bias and local genome context constrain adaptive paths](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42806813/), online 29 September 2026, describe 95 yeast chemostat replicates **in total** across three nutrient limitations. Their abstract associates local genomic context with the mutation modality observed at genes. This motivates context-specific mutation-supply comparators; it does not supply independently observed mutation births or a native SBW25 test.

The next useful assessment is full text and data availability for the cross-feeding study, including experimental units, sampling, denominators, and whether origins and losses are measured independently. Both abstracts report selected evolutionary outcomes; neither resolves the existing barcode crosswalk or the study's missing biological measurements. Search coverage was partial; no global absence or novelty claim is made.
