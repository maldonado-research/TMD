# Candidate cancer-domain observation and falsification contract

Status: prospective design/methods candidate. No route catalog, model system, partner, protocol, sample size, study registration or intervention has been selected or commissioned.

## Units and independent measurements

1. **Matched background and context.** A qualified laboratory and biological/statistical reviewers would select a defined epithelial cell or organoid system. A known common ancestor, relevant genome/allelic state and founding history must be preserved across contexts. Donor, animal, clone, culture, batch and occasion identifiers distinguish biological replication from nested subsamples. Human tissue maps across different ages and donors cannot be relabeled as randomized context-matched cultures.
2. **Fixed categorical events.** R1,R2,R3 must be disjoint, predefined molecular-acquisition catalogs on a fixed reference and explicit ancestral state. A newly arising event is assigned through a locked mapping independent of its later expansion. Multi-hit, unresolved, imported or pre-existing states require an explicit Other/origin classification rather than arbitrary assignment. The ordered axis s=(1,0,-1) needs an independently chosen biological/model rationale before outcomes; changing its gene order after seeing data changes the hypothesis. No actual catalog or axis rationale is established here. Mutation signatures may overlap and are not assumed to be distinct causal routes. TMD natural dominance means relative successful-route propensity in the chosen context; it is not heterozygote phenotype dominance.
3. **Supply unit and exposure.** Independently obtain context-matched relative new-origin supply q_ci, with a justified eligible-event definition, division/time exposure, ancestry evidence and its own uncertainty. Sequence reads, variant allele fractions, sampled tissue area, clone abundance and repeated observations of inherited variants are not independent new mutation births. Sequence opportunity, repair, local context, copying errors, mosaic starting material and copy-number changes need explicit treatment. Reporter-to-native and culture-to-human transfer cannot be assumed.
4. **Lineage outcome.** Prespecify a heritable lineage fate/eligibility threshold, observation period and a rule selecting at most one categorical outcome per independent founded culture for a proposed conditional-triad analysis. Record every culture, no-event result, Other outcome, censoring and detection failure. Genotypes alone do not establish malignant transformation. Growth, persistence, differentiated cell loss and spatial competition must be observed separately where relevant. This candidate does not provide a valid statistical count law until the selection and observation rule is justified.
5. **Recovery and controls.** Independent route-specific known-input controls must address capture, viable recovery, molecular classification, allele/copy-number state, missing lineages and technical transport. Controls need to resemble the biological states actually measured. Absolute capture does not retrospectively count extinct lineages. A probability inferred from nominal geometry, a passing aliquot split or selected survivors is not calibrated cancer-cell recovery.

## Restricted prediction and falsifier

For positive q and positive eligible conditional-triad probabilities p, the candidate transported form is

    p_ci proportional to q_ci*exp(theta*1{i=R2}+eta_c*s_i), s=(1,0,-1).
    J_c=p_c2^2*q_c1*q_c3/(p_c1*p_c3*q_c2^2)=exp(2theta).

This is a proposed use of the project's restricted log-linear form, not an already established cancer equation. Every positive triad can be fitted within one context. With C contexts the shared theta contributes C-1 restrictions relative to unrestricted positive triads. At least one complete context/block must be held out under a prospectively justified design; no actual number of contexts or cultures is chosen here.

For a full held-out probability forecast, training must fix theta and a prespecified covariate or independent assay must fix the new context's eta before selected outcomes are revealed. Refitting eta on held-out outcomes tests only the weaker curve restriction. Model selection, route ordering, category boundaries and context choice cannot be optimized on held-out selected outcomes. Zero/missing routes require a separately justified branch rather than invented pseudocounts.

Incompatible common corrected curvature across independently measured contexts would contradict this restricted prediction under the admitted observation and transport assumptions. Failure of a prospectively fixed held-out calibration or score criterion would reject that forecasting specification. Performance no better than meaningful competing forecasts would provide no incremental predictive support; it would not by itself disprove every TMD formulation. Statistical rejection requires eligible denominators, independent observations, justified simultaneous uncertainty and a prospectively reviewed error/stopping design; no new numerical decision threshold is supplied here. The current TMD confidence/score procedures cannot be copied into a cancer dataset merely because it has counts. Compatibility is not proof of a new mechanism.

## Strong competing explanations

- **Supply:** unchanged or altered standard mutation generation, sequence context, repair state and mutation opportunity can change routes without a new dominance force.
- **Growth/selection:** lineage birth, reproduction, differentiation, loss, selection, drift and spatial competition can change detectable abundance while mutation generation stays fixed. Mutant clones abundant in normal epithelium need not be malignant; mutant neighbors may alter nascent-lesion survival.
- **Origins and genotype state:** pre-existing mosaic lineages, acquired second hits, allelic loss/copy changes and shared ancestry can produce the same detected genotype with different origin histories.
- **Recovery:** route-dependent sampling, detection thresholds, sequencing/copy-number ascertainment, viability and control-transfer mismatch can mimic a restricted endpoint pattern.
- **Heterogeneity:** donor, background, passage, occasion and latent culture parameters can induce apparent context relations. Pooling nested tissue samples can overstate independent information.

Compare supply alone, supply with independently measured growth/survival and recovery, the restricted model and a suitable flexible context/background model using the same training information and eligible outcomes. Success would support a predictive restriction in the tested model system; separating causal mutation production from these rivals requires independent measurements or scientifically justified interventions. No intervention is proposed by this document.

## Exact mimic and scope

Let expected recovered endpoint descendant masses be proportional to q_i*a_ci. Choosing a_c=(t_c,u,1/t_c) gives

    J_c=a_c2^2/(a_c1*a_c3)=u^2,

because the normalizer and supply cancel. Yield can use g=a,r=1; recovery can use g=1,r=a/max(a), with valid recovery probabilities. These g values are relative expected terminal yields incorporating growth and retention/loss. Values below1 are not fractional realized offspring. Multiplying every g in a context by a sufficiently large common positive scale makes all at least1 and leaves the normalized shares unchanged; total endpoint mass need not remain the same. Both examples have identical normalized expected endpoint mass shares while q stays fixed. This does not imply identical count variances, full distributions, selected lineage probabilities or first-arrival timing. A future lineage design needs its own observation model. The worked exact examples only expose a confounding boundary for endpoint summaries.

## Unmet inputs and cancer outcome boundary

The system/background, independent replicate unit, actual catalogs, axis rationale, contexts, new-origin supply, ancestry, division exposure, lineage growth/loss, observation/censoring rule, capture/recovery and transport bounds, held-out eta rule, laboratory scope, feasibility, data/privacy permissions and registered statistical protocol remain unresolved. These requirements are not new filled fields in the existing26-field bacterial registry.

The human2018 article used844 samples from nine donors and deferred detailed methods to supplementary MethodsS1–S7, unacquired here. The mouse2021 article reports tumors and biopsies nested in mice, combines experiments with separate simulations and leaves analogous human tumor elimination unknown. Its dominant-negative DN-Maml1 construct is a molecular perturbation name, not evidence for TMD. These studies motivate the measurement distinctions and competing explanations; they supply no transferable TMD cancer parameter.

Cancer prevention would require an independently justified prevention endpoint and evidence that an intervention reduces disease risk with acceptable harms. This candidate supplies neither. Cancer-associated mutation, detectable clone expansion, early lesion persistence and clinical cancer incidence remain separate. Current progress consists of source assessment, a conditional falsifiable translation question and reproducible synthetic algebra.
