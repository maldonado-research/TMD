The [bounded reopening plan](SIZE_BOUNDED_REPLAY_PLAN.json) sets stop conditions rather than assuming that these originals fit a small download budget. A future metadata decision stage is capped at three logical calls/2 MiB, then a separately allocated manifest stage at eight calls/2 MiB. Any subsequently justified bounded payload phase has an 18 MiB payload cap, an 8 MiB per-object cap and a 128 MiB expanded working cap. The current original-file minima fail those caps. No such acquisition is made in R26. An authenticated extract could qualify only if an authorized source-side process can provide complete required rows/headers/overlap evidence with parent-file hashes and a reproducible outcome-independent selection rule. Availability of that facility is unknown; a webpage preview or truncated response cannot substitute for it. A larger complete-file acquisition would need its own justified resource plan.

The [truth decision](TRUTH_ADMISSION_DECISION.json) separates three targets:

1. **Code reproduction:** eligible in principle after complete authenticated inputs, environment and semantics. This yields conditional descriptions of the authors' reference rows. Independent biological truth is not necessary to reproduce arithmetic; missing truth and acquisition cost make this a lower research priority, not an invalid software task.
2. **Independent genotype accuracy:** not admitted. Shared PTA/bulk training positives, linked-read-score artifact labels, low-burden cord-blood proxy labels and signature-refit artifact estimates have different evidence roles. The article explicitly states that exact artifact counts were unknown in some experiments. The clone-shared recovery frame is conditioned on preceding-clone SMuRF CLONAL/PASS_QC and VAF >0.2. The callable subset is additionally selected using PTATO outcomes and global variant membership. Neither is a neutral, completely enumerated accuracy frame. A sensitivity-only target would need independently supported positive truth and an explicit eligible denominator; precision, specificity and full confusion matrices need their own appropriate negative/unknown ascertainment. One missing target does not invalidate every conceivable narrower target.
3. **Native origin or TMD inference:** not admitted. Even accurate genotype truth would not supply independent ancestry/episode labels, standing-variant origin negatives, missing/lost lineages, all-eligible-origin inclusion or transport to the target system. Expected culture-day burdens and agreement of spectra do not supply those observations.

The article's out-of-training AML/cord-blood in-silico analysis remains a useful separate design, but its recombined records and mutated contexts are not four additional biological trials. Table S2 marks the FANCC PTA sample and its predecessor as training samples, and Figure2.R includes them. The other three PTA rows say Training=No; that does not authenticate untouched donor/lineage/variant evaluation or adaptation. No leakage claim is made solely from overlap. The four PTA measurements are paired and nested in AHH-1 expansion histories and repair backgrounds; a binomial interval treating every locus or plotted sample as an independent trial is not justified here.

Before any replay, preserve the R25 [ordered semantic contract](../genotype_denominators/GENOTYPE_REPLAY_CONTRACT.json): exact source row indices and sample/variant keys; duplicate and multiallelic audit; contig/build and interval conventions; the WT label reversal; predecessor and training maps; missing/QC states; strict predecessor VAF and PTA VAF/cutoff boundaries; the PTATO and SCAN2 override orders and rescued rows; chromosome 17 removal; global versus sample-specific membership; and pre-display FAIL_VAF states. Keep category-specific numbers of contributing samples, zero-denominator groups, literal sparse means, zero-completed means and pooled descriptive fractions distinct. Actual differences remain uncomputed. Altering the frame to sample-specific PTATO PASS/FAIL remains outcome selection and supplies no independent truth.
