# Which grapevine pair patterns survive the choice of source table?

The Tempranillo study reports the original 50 vines twice. Table S4 supplies individual unphased genotype calls on two platforms at 56 selected SNVs. Tables S1 and S5 assign each vine to a named genotype class and supply that class’s representative profile. Table S3 also records the original 20 classes at 158 variants. For the additional 135 vines, the published workbook supplies class assignments and profiles. Individual platform readouts for those additional vines are unavailable in these tables. The initial panel includes 35 whole-genome variant-discovery vines and 15 vines genotyped from sequencing data. The original study selected the 56 markers using the initial phylogeny. [Primary report and supplement](https://doi.org/10.1093/hr/uhaf237)

Both platforms agree on all 2,800 unordered S4 genotype comparisons. Joining genotypes to vine names nevertheless gives nine differences between S4 and the corresponding S3/S5 class-profile entries. Four GT-16 vines are C:C in S4 versus C:T in the template at B_chr08_00848520. One GT-11 vine is G:T versus T:T at B_chr08_21422396. Four GT-09 or GT-10 vines are A:G versus G:T at B_chr10_05943585. Five of the nine change a homozygous/heterozygous indicator. The comparison sorts alleles in unphased genotype strings, including a G:A entry in GT-20. The source does not identify the reason for these differences. S3 and S5 agree for all 20 original classes at their 56 overlapping SNVs. All 23 S5 profiles have calls throughout the 56 positions. Three inferred ancestral genotype calls in S3 are unassigned, including one of the 56 selected positions. The pair comparison uses no ancestral assignment. [Native workbook cross-check and discrepancy list](workbook_result.json)

For 56-marker binary profiles there are 1,540 pairs. When the first 50 vines are represented by their S5 templates, expanding to all 185 introduces a previously unseen joint state at 12 pairs. Representing the 50 with S4 and the additional 135 with the S5 templates raises that count to 68. Both presentations give zero pairs with all four binary joint states. Excluding the three discrepant loci leaves 53 markers and 1,378 pairs and gives exactly the same individual binary rows in either presentation. The initial panel then contains 81 pairs with two joint states and 1,297 with three. The expanded panel has 69 pairs with two and 1,309 with three. All 12 changes are from two to three observed states. The additional genotype classes GT-20, GT-21 and GT-22 account for eight, three and one changes respectively. For B_chr02_19352340 and B_chr08_07685896, Tempranillo-001 and Tempranillo-014 represent 00 and 11 in the selection panel. The later Tempranillo-121 (GT-21) adds 01. A one denotes a heterozygous call. The 135 additional vines contain 14 distinct assigned genotype classes, and 32 of their original 56 markers vary under this coding.

Four observed combinations of full genotype labels can mean something different when a locus has three observed genotype states. Combining S4 for the original vines with S5 for the others introduces A:G, G:T and G:G at the discrepant chromosome 10 position. At B_chr09_06530273 and B_chr10_05943585, the combined records contain T:T/A:G, C:T/A:G, T:T/G:T and C:T/G:T, as well as T:T/G:G. The first two come from S4 and the next two from the class-profile assignments. The A:G versus G:T change at chromosome 10 disappears on coding either as heterozygous. The numerical result applies to the stated binary assay and the retained loci. It provides no general claim of compatibility across source encodings, mutation order or cellular lineages.

The independent verifier reads the original XML with Python’s standard library and checks its workbook hash. Run it from this directory with the original XLSX obtained from the study’s supplementary materials.

```sh
python3 -B workbook_oracle.py /path/to/supplement.xlsx | diff - workbook_result.json
```
