# Stationary-phase genetic exchange: a route-entry boundary

Mutation by Natural Dominance · R000008 · 7 October 2026, America/Los_Angeles

The primary article behind the October 7 abstract screen is now accessible and has been read at full-main-text depth. It provides a concrete **horizontal genetic transfer** alternative to mutation-only entry of heritable variants. It does not provide a matched SBW25 Wsp/Aws/Mws panel, establish that transfer occurs in the proposed TMD experiment, or supply a transferable parameter for that experiment. All 26 actual-study fields remain unresolved.

Payne, Plevka, Bollback and Guet (2026), *Herd immunity underlies homologous recombination in stationary phase bacteria*, Molecular Biology and Evolution, [10.1093/molbev/msag238](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msag238), [PMC13633115](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13633115/). ## What the experiment measures

The authors engineered two *E. coli* MG1655 strains. Sus carries a cyan-fluorescence/spectinomycin-resistance chromosomal cassette and a nontargeting Cas9 plasmid. Immu carries a yellow-fluorescence/kanamycin-resistance cassette, a red plasmid marker, and engineered Cas9 spacers against P1. The resistance cassettes are separated by 389 kbp; the source states P1 packages approximately 100 kb. The design lets imported donor-marker DNA be distinguished from resistance acquired without the second fluorescence marker.

The main mixture starts near 95% Immu/5% Sus with P1vir at nominal MOI 0.1. Culture measurements are reported in triplicate, with samples from evolving cultures at several times. Colony totals and repeated times are not extra independently founded cultures. Kanamycin and spectinomycin selection are imposed on sampling plates, not during the main coculture growth. **Chloramphenicol plasmid-maintenance selection remains present**, so “nonselective” does not mean that every antibiotic or maintenance effect is absent. Calcium supports phage adsorption; citrate on sampling plates is intended to suppress further adsorption. Selection plates incubate for 48 hours.

Double-resistant colonies expressing both cyan and yellow markers are classified as recombinant CFUs. Double-resistant colonies expressing one cassette's fluorescence are classified as mutant CFUs. Initial constructs were sequence-verified, but this main-text reading did not find molecular sequence validation of every scored colony. About 1.5% of recombinant CFUs lack the red plasmid fluorescence; the authors give alternative plasmid-exchange explanations. Preserve the phenotypic classification and its limits rather than treating fluorescence as an infallible molecular origin assay.

The source reports recombinant CFUs accumulating as cultures enter stationary phase, with approximately **380 times as many recovered recombinant as mutant CFUs at 24 hours**. That is a comparison of recovered descendants in this assay. It is not a direct count of independent molecular mutation and transfer events, an unbiased genome-wide rate ratio, or a general bacterial constant. Clone growth, survival, donor/recipient dynamics, phenotypic classification and recovery can affect both arms.

The 1:2 dilution experiment gives approximately one generation of renewed growth and detects subsequent recombinant accumulation. The article derives a reporter-locus quantity of 1.97×10^-7 per generation from 689 detected CFU-derived occurrences per mL divided by roughly 3.5×10^9 CFU/mL, then extrapolates across the chromosome under an approximately uniform-recovery assumption. The detailed supplementary calculation and raw measurements were **not acquired or independently reproduced**. An author-labelled event estimate must not be upgraded into directly observed independent birth events or an authenticated native SBW25 rate.

## Causal evidence and remaining limits

Spacer/control comparisons, donor–recipient/phage coexistence, nutrient pulses and recombination-gene interventions support the reported transfer mechanism in this engineered ecosystem. They are stronger evidence than an endpoint correlation alone. The experiment does not isolate every mediation pathway or prove ecological generality.

The knockout assays use specified Keio collection stocks, plasmid immunity and donor-grown phage lysate, with triplicate assays. The ΔrecA strain is grown longer because it grows more slowly. The main text reports no detected transductants in ΔrecA, ΔrecB and ΔrecG, reduced recovery in ΔrecF, and no significant reduction in ΔruvA. These are assay outcomes with detection and growth limits. No detected colony is not proof of an exactly zero molecular rate. Stock/background matching, complementation, individual CFU/MOI values and the supplementary model/controls have not been independently authenticated in this reading. The proposed RecG–stationary-phase regulatory link remains a mechanistic suggestion rather than demonstrated transcriptional mediation.

The article uses pairwise variance tests followed by two-sample t-tests with three observations per group. Some comparisons involve serial time points or nutrient-pulse branches from evolving cultures. The raw experimental lineage, pairing, exclusions and multiple-comparison structure are needed before independently reproducing an inferential claim. No new p-value, effect interval or power result is supplied here.

## Consequence for TMD

“Natural dominance” remains the operational propensity of a defined heritable route to yield a specified successful outcome. A common endpoint allele can arise through de novo mutation, import of an allele already present in a donor, or lineage sorting from standing variation. These origins have different denominators and histories, even when the final molecular allele is identical.

Where independent event intensities and a justified marking/establishment model apply, source bookkeeping can be written schematically as

    λ_i(t) = B(t) μ_i(t) A_i^mutation(t) F_i^mutation(t)
           + H_i(t) A_i^transfer(t) F_i^transfer(t).

H denotes incoming route-bearing genetic material per time in the specified recipient/frame. It is not a mutation probability per division. This is ordinary additive cause-specific bookkeeping, **not a new theorem or a fitted model**. Donor/phage feedback, shared lineages and clone interactions can invalidate a simple independent-rate approximation. Endpoint CFUs do not separately identify either term.

A mutation-only baseline q is inadequate if unmeasured donor-mediated entries contribute materially. The test must either justify a closed mutation-only domain with source/ecology controls, or explicitly define and measure combined entry with origin-specific categories and comparators. Do not relabel imported alleles as newly created mutations, estimate the entry baseline from the selected winners, or invent a finite transfer bound from this one E. coli–P1 study. Its reported fold values and engineered phage–host/regulator parameters cannot be transferred to SBW25.

Useful discriminating observations include initial donor/recipient haplotypes and inoculum diversity; temporal recipient genotypes with linked donor markers; phage or other DNA-vector evidence; appropriate no-donor/no-vector controls; and growth, survival and recovery measurements. Phenotype gates must distinguish imported-marker expression, mutation-derived resistance, Other, unresolved and missing outcomes. A shared-θ W/A/M forecast still needs independently measured eligible supply and frozen held-out predictions. This source neither fills the registry nor tests that restriction.
