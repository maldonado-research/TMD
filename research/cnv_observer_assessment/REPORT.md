# R38 source assessment: what a genetically checked CNV reporter admits

Lauer et al. (2018), [*Single-cell copy number variant detection reveals the dynamics and diversity of adaptation*](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3000069), supplies a distinct, implemented example of fluorescent mutation-class observation with molecular checks and separate competitive-fitness measurements. It does **not** supply independently authenticated mutation births or single-new-mutant establishment. The useful admission boundary is that molecular validation of selected late clones does not validate every earlier reporter call, its acquisition time, or its ancestry.

This primary study was not present in the checked TMD source ledgers. The comparison is to R28's already-inspected *E. coli* YFP-MutL source, not an independent replication of that source. The present study concerns *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*, the GAP1 copy-number class, and nitrogen-limited chemostats. It provides no SBW25 W/A/M calibration, native panel, biological fit, or admitted study/planning input.

## Actual measurement chain

The reporter is a constitutively expressed mCitrine construct placed 1,118 bases upstream of GAP1. The authors separately constructed copy-number controls, including one- and two-copy reporter controls at neutral loci. Engineered copy-number controls produced distinct single-cell fluorescence distributions, normalized by forward scatter. PCR and Sanger sequencing confirmed reporter integration. These controls concern reporter construction and signal response; they are not independent witnesses to each later spontaneous mutation.

The original evolution experiment comprised 26 GAP1-reporter populations: nine glutamine-limited, nine urea-limited and eight glucose-limited populations, plus six neutral-locus control populations. Each flow-cytometry sample measured 100,000 cells, approximately every eight generations. Those cells are observations nested within an evolving population, not 100,000 independent mutation events. The separately derived barcode experiment comprised six glutamine-limited populations; detailed barcode sequencing of CNV-enriched fractions concerned two of them, bc01 and bc02.

| Measurement | What the source actually supports | What remains unadmitted |
|---|---|---|
| Fluorescent signal | Copy-number response of engineered controls and an evolving CNV-enriched fraction | A complete mutation-event census, all molecular routes, or first mutation acquisition |
| First reported detection, `T_up` | Threshold crossing by a population's apparent CNV fraction | Birth time of an individual mutation or an independently observed establishment time |
| Genome sequencing and Southern checks | Molecular support for CNVs in sampled, cultured clones and later population samples | Molecular truth for every earlier flow/FACS call, a parent-to-daughter event join, or complete negatives |
| Barcode enrichment | Persistence and turnover of labelled CNV-enriched lineages, with stated corrections and assumptions | One barcode equals one founder, one mutation, or one independent event |
| Pairwise clone competition | Separately measured clone fitness in the source glutamine-limited conditions | Survival/establishment probability of a newly arisen single mutant in the resident evolving population |

The Methods define `T_up` using **7.1%**, obtained as the mean plus one standard deviation of the apparent CNV fraction in evolving one-copy controls during generations 1–153. The experimental population must exceed that threshold. This is a population detection threshold based on control behavior. It is not a 7.1% event-level false-positive probability, a validated confidence bound, or proof that a mutation first occurred then. The reported `S_up` comes from the log ratio of the reporter-positive and reporter-negative fractions during an initial fitted expansion phase; it is an aggregate trajectory summary, not an independent mutation-supply measurement.

The authors selected 29 fluorescence-increased clones from generations 150 and 250 for whole-genome sequencing, with molecular copy-number and structure checks including Southern blotting. Competitive fitness was reported for a different denominator of **28** clones: 18 higher, eight without a significant fitness difference, and two lower relative fitness. These endpoints support genotype and context-specific fitness for sampled clones. They do not imply that all reporter-positive lineages are beneficial, or that selected clone recovery includes extinct or unobserved precursors.

For FACS-enriched fractions, the Methods describe 48 hours of growth before DNA preparation. The purity correction used isolated clones remeasured by flow cytometry, with at least 25 clones required for a subpopulation's false-positive calculation. That is a separate readout of the same fluorescent phenotype after culture, not an event-specific sequence truth set. It neither establishes a false-negative denominator nor independently authenticates the origin of a CNV. The whole-genome checks elsewhere in the article must not be silently substituted for this particular correction procedure.

The article explicitly notes that a barcode can be shared by multiple founding cells and that one complex CNV may result from multiple successive events. Its assignments of shared versus population-specific barcodes to preexisting versus new CNVs are probabilistic. Thus, barcode absence in the other population cannot certify an origin-negative ancestor, and a barcode count cannot be promoted to an independent mutation count. A high mutation supply is inferred from the selected lineages; the inspected material does not separately measure both supply and establishment with independent event-level denominators.

## Falsifiable boundary and next admissible artifact

If a future TMD analysis equates a reporter-labelled lineage with one newly born, independently established mutation, that claim requires more than the source's validation chain. A falsifiable reporter-admission check would join each sampled reporter-positive **and reporter-negative** unit to independent molecular genotype, its ancestral state, sampling time, culture/selection history and the relevant denominator. A true target variant in the negative set, a reporter-positive unit without the target variant, or more than one event/founder associated with a label would refute the corresponding perfect-classification or one-to-one interpretation. Such checks can still support a narrower, calibrated classification claim when their actual uncertainty and sampling frame are retained.

For an origin claim, a separate earlier parent/descendant molecular record and a justified bound on mutations predating observation are required. For establishment, the survival criterion must be observed before the later recovery/classification procedure, with lost-lineage and exposure records. Neither an increase in aggregate frequency nor a pairwise fitness coefficient substitutes for that earlier observer. These are conditional admission requirements; no assay is selected, no experiment is registered or commissioned, and no numerical native design field is filled.

Further source acquisition is justified only for a named, bounded artifact that resolves a gate—for example, a clone-level fluorescence-to-sequence crosswalk with selection and missingness metadata, or actual DNA-genotyped positive and negative sorting controls. An additional endpoint table or another barcode trajectory alone would not resolve native origin, independent supply, or establishment. The inspected OSF metadata narrows the artifact inventory, but file content, relevant version, artifact-level joins and deposit reuse terms still require authentication before numerical reuse. The article's open license does not establish the terms of the linked deposit.

## Preserved discrepancies and scope

The Results/Figure 5 label the last barcode time as generation 270, while the FACS Methods list 265. The original evolution Results describe 267 generations, while Methods describe 270. These labels are preserved; no row-level timing correction is inferred. The 29 sequenced-clone and 28 competitive-fitness denominators remain separate, and the barcode strain is separately derived from the original reporter strain. “No significant fitness defect” for source reporter controls is not a quantitative equivalence bound, a statement about every CNV spectrum, or a transport guarantee.
