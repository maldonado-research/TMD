# Independent source and methods review

Mutation by Natural Dominance · R000006 · 7 October 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)

**Accepted within the stated public-source audit scope.** This is independent internal AI-assisted review, not external peer review. The package reproduces specific released figure-data calculations and preserves their biological and sampling limits. It does not supply a matched W/A/M panel, establish a native mutation rate, reproduce the MSS likelihood/interval fit, resolve the 26 actual-study fields, or confirm TMD.

The reviewer read the original audit code, complete derived measurement output, original notes, source ledger, contract gaps, privacy/license assessment, acquisition and verification receipts, the current TMD formulation and prospective registry, and primary Farr Methods/Results/Discussion plus embedded S2–S4 captions. A separately acquired Foster methodological chapter was read in full in the relevant fluctuation-model sections. Hall's original FALCOR methods article was inspected at abstract/metadata depth only; its attempted HTML full text was a browser-check page. No FALCOR service, raw cytometry gating, qPCR fluorescence fit or supporting PDF was executed or reprocessed.

The final pinned ZIP's advertised MD5 and acquired SHA-256 were verified. Full author-code replay passes **986 counted source checks**: 504 Figure 4A numeric/plot, 144 formula texts, 144 time-course, 96 competition-coefficient, and 98 transcript arithmetic/primer-mapping checks. Replay output matches the final stored derived JSON. Those checks are not independent biological trials or tests of TMD.

Separate source oracles use a different raw-ZIP XML cell reader, exact averaged Ct differences, direct string-anchor searches rather than the audited regex caller, and the independently implemented root frequency reconstruction. They verify **12 qPCR replicate mappings**, all **24 selected amplicon identities**, **190 diagnostic Fig. 4A reads in 24 culture groups**, and exact agreement of **24 reconstructed terminal-frequency values**. The genotype diagnostic requires the declared marker context and quality gate; unresolved/missing reads remain unresolved/missing and never replace the source's counts. It is not a comprehensive variant caller.

Review caught and corrected the initial candidate's reversed 5′/3′ labels. Source setup wells B/F assay nlpD5′; C/G assay nlpD3′. Thus the supplied proxy is `2^(Ct5′−Ct3′)`; the numerical implementation was already correct and no frequency or fold contrast changed. Reconstructed geometric- and arithmetic-mean linear-proxy ratios are 5.9603 and 6.1488. The published approximately-fourfold caption and approximately-sixfold main text remain an unresolved reporting discrepancy; a 3.8307 ratio of log means is not a linear fold effect or an established explanation of that discrepancy. Author attribution was checked against the primary author list, including **Christina Vasileiou**.

The review also required source-faithful descriptions of six **individual** transformants per genotype reused across two occasions, and of J as a **culture dilution fraction**. Separate construction events and an overall culture sampling fraction are not established by that wording. Shared transformants alone do not prove that freshly founded cultures are dependent; the preserved grouping permits a justified conditional or blocked analysis.

An independent raw-workbook check confirms the unresolved occasion-1 SBW25 replicate-5 row: H=7 candidate colonies, M=8 sequenced colonies, L=2 confirmations. Additional plates or another counting scope may explain this, but a closed seven-colony sampling pool is not authenticated. The 94-read/95-workbook-confirmation gap is also retained. Exact arithmetic replay does not establish a binomial or hypergeometric sampling law.

The 118.97 pooled descriptive terminal-frequency ratio and the article's 58.33 MSS mutation-rate point-estimate ratio refer to distinct estimands. Clone growth, survival, plating, genotype confirmation, reporter fitness and the rate-estimation model remain relevant. Supplied competition coefficients are relative performance proxies, not establishment probabilities; supplied transcript quantities are assay proxies, not native mutation supply. PsrA deletion is a controlled intervention showing reporter-assay dependence on regulator genotype; sole transcriptional mediation or a specific chemical mechanism remains unproved.

The accompanying original methods note gives a sign-checked, conditional algebraic supply-transfer extension: when `q_native ∝ q_proxy exp(ζ)`, `θ_proxy = θ_native + v·ζ` with `v=(-1/2,1,-1/2)`. Independently justified supply and recovery contrast widths, and their statistical failure budgets if estimated, add conservatively. This is existing sensitivity analysis, not a new theorem. Single-marker C565T data do not identify three-route ζ or finite transport bounds.

Public-safe scope was reviewed: original notes/scripts, minimum source-integer provenance, derived numerical summaries, hashes, attribution and receipts. Raw ZIP/workbooks/reads/GenBank/RTF, full article text, operating-system metadata, laboratory machine paths and operator metadata are excluded. No private archive file, personal document or credential was used or authorized for publication.

The next scientific priority is the source-to-measurement/native-transport contract, including culture/block ancestry, exact colony-confirmation frame, division/population history, genotype visibility and recovery, then a forecast frozen before matched held-out W/A/M outcomes. The newly screened October 3/5 papers were separately checked at abstract-only depth; they do not change this admission decision.
