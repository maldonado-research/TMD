## CP roots and coverage

For k>0, P_p[Bin(n,p)>=k] increases continuously from zero to one. Its root at the positive allocated tail probability therefore defines the CP lower endpoint. For k<n, P_p[Bin(n,p)<=k] decreases continuously from one to zero; its root defines the upper endpoint. The candidate's reversed bisection comparison for this second tail is correct. Support cases k=0 and k=n give exact lower zero and upper one. Equality at a dyadic midpoint can collapse the bracket. Otherwise exact polynomial comparisons maintain the two root brackets, each of width at most 2^-B. Selecting the lower bracket's lower endpoint and the upper bracket's upper endpoint enlarges the ideal interval; it never shrinks it.

The tail recurrence multiplies integer polynomial terms and checks exact divisibility before advancing. Its direct and complemented forms agree with independent polynomial sums, including p=0 and p=1. CP inversion controls each marginal's two noncoverage events by its two allocated tails. There are 3C fixed marginal coordinates, each with tail allocation alpha/(6C), so the union bound controls their simultaneous noncoverage by alpha. Neither dependence among a context's categories nor dependence across contexts requires an additional independence assumption for this unconditional union bound, provided the required marginal count laws are valid.

Random qualifying totals need special care. The requisite law is the binomial/multinomial marginal conditional on that context's own qualifying total and the independently frozen prediction information. Averaging its conditional guarantee over that total gives unconditional marginal control. The mathematical coverage extension may assign a zero-qualification context the whole simplex; the implementation instead reports unevaluable and issues no certificate. This controls the probability of an incorrect issued certificate across complete fixed-study repetitions. It does **not** automatically give conditional coverage after restricting to all-positive totals, a jointly observed vector of totals, issued reports or favorable decisions across dependent contexts. The author added this explicit distinction to the README and result's coverage regime after review. A stronger jointly conditional law or justified independence would be required for those additional conditioning claims.

An independent finite four-category IID founder example verifies the qualifying-total mixture argument. It does not establish that an actual assay, missingness process, selected-isolate spectrum, fluctuation jackpot or correlated descendants satisfy the required law. A complete endpoint ledger is necessary for the declared frame and remains insufficient to prove IID sampling. The underlying target is observed W/A/M conditional on qualification; the excluded categories do not become an all-category prediction.

## Precision-planning guarantee

The claimed worst-case half-width bound is valid **for three categories**; the coefficient is not a dimension-free formula. Apply Hoeffding to the CP-defining tail: if the empirical proportion is v=k/n, each ideal CP endpoint is within r=sqrt(log(1/tau)/(2n)) of v after clipping. Outward root brackets add at most d_CP=2^-B, so every point in the box/simplex region obeys |p_i-v_i|<=r+d_CP.

Let a be the vector of logarithm-interval midpoints. Subtract its median from every coordinate; the score difference is unchanged because p and v each sum to one. In a triad, the sum of absolute centered coefficients is exactly max(a)-min(a). Consequently |a dot (p-v)| is at most range(a)*(r+d_CP). Equivalently, a three-coordinate zero-sum difference has total variation equal to its largest absolute coordinate; this identity was checked exhaustively on a rational simplex grid. It would not have the same constant in a general higher-dimensional simplex.

The candidate's R is at least the midpoint coefficient range, and D bounds the greatest coefficient interval width. For any p, replacing a coefficient midpoint by its interval endpoint changes the objective by at most D/2, since nonnegative p sums to one. Hence the computed context interval, including independently optimized lower and upper coefficients, has half-width at most R*(r+d_CP)+D/2. Summing with nonnegative frozen weights and adding d_report for final outward rounding gives exactly the stated finite-precision allowance and stochastic width term. The logarithm upper bound L_up is certified and no smaller than log(1/tau).

After subtracting finite precision from the requested target, the positive residual budget r_budget is divided into positive pre-outcome shares s_c summing to one. The rational ceiling n_c>=ceil[L_up*(w_c R_c)^2/(2*(r_budget s_c)^2)] guarantees w_c R_c sqrt(L/(2n_c))<=r_budget s_c. Summation proves the worst-case guarantee for every valid count vector meeting those qualifying counts, with no outcome-based optimization. A minimum of one qualifying outcome is a conservative requirement even for a zero-weight or zero-range context. Fifty-five accepted coarse-precision guides were independently tested by enumerating all selected small count vectors and explicit vertices; six too-fine precision requests were rejected. These finite checks supplement this proof rather than establishing it by sampling.

The required n_c are **qualifying counts**, not the fixed full-founder totals. The latter need not achieve them. The stored 0.05-nat illustration requires 527 and 921 qualifying outcomes, exceeding the 500-unit prototype cap, and explicitly reports both unsupported. This result is a mathematical guide, not an executable study in this engine, an achieved effect, a success-gate choice or a power result. ## Domain, integrity and remaining research limits

The reviewed JSON domain requires exact rational strings, positive forecast components, exact vector and weight sums, bounded integer counts, the full exclusion ledger, explicit assertions, and a matching frozen-plan digest. Inputs with floats, bool counts, nonfinite constants, ambiguous duplicate keys, zero denominators, unsupported precision or empty probability regions are rejected. Typed caches prevent a previously accepted Fraction/int call from allowing an equal-valued inadmissible bool or float to bypass validation. The reviewer checked guards after warming the caches, not only with empty caches.

Full-frame, work and argument caps are explicit implementation bounds. They are not scientific sample-size recommendations or universal wall-clock/memory guarantees. Invalid or unevaluable inputs produce no certificate; root bisection is not silently coarsened to force execution. Digests prove byte integrity, not laboratory registration, ownership of biological data or independence from outcomes.

The R3 genotype registry, assay transfer, independent denominators, founder law, contextual range, meaningful effect thresholds and registration are still unresolved. The implementation evaluates the realized rational frozen forecast under asserted laws; it does not identify latent mutation probabilities, inheritance, selection, drift, route establishment or recovery effects separately. New code removes a numerical obstacle to a conditional observed-outcome test. It supplies neither evidence that the hypothesis is biologically true nor a new mathematical discovery.
