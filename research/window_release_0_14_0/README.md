# Mutation by Natural Dominance — Can mutation spectra predict the first recorded substitution class?

Published 21 November 2026 Pacific in the Mutation by Natural Dominance report and methods series as version 0.14.0. A spectrum learned from other yeast families beat equal odds for every one of 36 possible first-class assignments in one study. A second study allowed either forecast to win, and an exact random-window check gave limited evidence of special early timing. The plant comparison tested 925 endpoint substitutions in 48 lines.
