## Two claim branches and an ordered decision

The eight gates in the worksheet are a dependency graph, rather than eight restatements of control components:

1. **G0 — system and resource eligibility.** Authenticate the material, actual scientific scope, reviewer expertise and available access. The review may refuse an unavailable system without purchasing data.
2. **O1 — endpoint and independent-unit frame.** Admit molecular endpoint adjudication, all outcome categories, inclusion/missingness and a defensible marginal count law. A plate label or a field of view is not automatically a biological replicate.
3. **O2 — independent operational predictors.** Admit the baseline/recovery inputs and earlier establishment measurement needed by the existing algorithm. Operational prediction still needs those real inputs even when their mechanistic interpretation is withheld.
4. **O3 — complete frozen forecasting capability.** Show that the unchanged TMD rule and each comparator can supply the same complete, positive target-context W/A/M vector from permitted independent information. A scalar mutation rate or curvature does not supply that vector.
5. **O4 — evaluation and decision readiness.** Admit an untouched evaluation frame, resource/precision plan, numeric implementation, context weights, meaningful-effect gates, error allocation, fixed stopping and independently dated freeze.
6. **B1 — independent acquisition and exposure truth.** Separately authenticate route-resolved native episodes, ancestors and exposure, including inherited/pre-existing variants, repeated acquisition, transfer and invisible-loss limits.
7. **B2 — native inclusion and origin calibration.** Validate the complete calling/inclusion predicate against independent origin-positive, inherited origin-negative/variant-positive and absent-variant truth. Retain joint failures, uncallability and missing truth.
8. **B3 — biological transport and interpretation.** In addition to valid observed evaluation, admit matched transport, route aggregation and the observation/generative model appropriate to the exact biological claim. A biological full-vector claim requires more than diagonal recovery or a J-only contrast bound.

O1–O4 follow G0. B1 can be assessed after G0/O1; B2 additionally needs O2; B3 needs B2/O3/O4. Failure of B1 does not logically prohibit a correctly scoped observed prediction. It does prohibit treating that prediction as independently measured native-origin evidence. Neither branch is admitted now. The native planning worksheet can remain unfilled for a claim restricted to observed prediction; that distinction does not resolve the currently missing observed-prediction inputs. Conditional fields, such as a full biological observation map, remain NULL rather than being silently populated or imposed on a different estimand.

Each qualified response must say which claim is accepted, revised or refused; cite a versioned evidence location; state uncertainty and limitations; and identify the reviewer and qualifications. A gate cannot be accepted merely because a field has a non-NULL answer. Dependent gates, evidence quality and reviewer decisions must also be satisfied. This packet's verifier checks consistency and preservation, not scientific validity or authority to admit a study.

## Can establishment be observed independently of recovery?

The practical review question is whether a known introduced lineage can be followed to an operational event at an earlier declared stage, with error assessed against a reference that does not use the final colony outcome as its truth. The operational event itself, its time and acceptable error remain choices for qualified reviewers; this packet supplies none.

Ask the team to return a traceable record from verified introduction identity through the earlier observation and terminal recovery, with separate states for failed introduction, observed establishment, observed non-establishment, lineage disappearance, observer failure and later assay failure. These are potential record states to adjudicate, not independent outcomes or a new mandatory endpoint category set. A disappearing track may represent motion, occlusion, detachment, washed-out cells, identity swaps or death. It must remain unknown unless an independent record distinguishes these causes. A molecular tag tracks an identity only to the extent that its stability, resolution and biological effects are validated; its colour does not define a W/A/M route.

The calibration deliverable should cross-tabulate independent reference status against observer call for every included challenge, retaining false events, missed events, unknown reference, wrong identity and no-call cases. It should describe coverage across the relevant spatial/physiological states, imaging depth, crowding and observation gaps, and show whether the marker, illumination or apparatus alters the ecology. Error and unknown-state bounds need a defensible independent-unit law and their eventual place in the existing study error budget. A second scorer using the same image sequence can assess scoring reproducibility; agreement does not establish independent biological truth or remove shared blind spots. Statistical independence of two signals is also not guaranteed merely because they use different instruments.

A destructive molecular assay can independently confirm an event in a sampled lineage but may prevent following that same lineage through terminal recovery. Parallel sacrificed cohorts could inform a distribution under justified matching; they are not paired truth for an unsampled individual's establishment and recovery. If no usable reference or error bound exists, refuse a separated establishment/recovery claim. Do not define establishment by terminal recovery to manufacture the missing calibration.

Known introductions solve a control's introduction denominator. They do not reveal the number or genotype of unseen native births. A control introduced after extraction only calibrates later steps. Even an accurately tracked introduced lineage may differ from a native lineage emerging at a later time within a resident scaffold; transport is a separate scientific question.

## A primary method sharpens the observer boundary

One narrow five-result paper-index search led to selected primary inspection of Enrico Bena et al. (2024), [*Real-time monitoring of replication errors' fate reveals the origin and dynamics of spontaneous mutations*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46950-0). The article combines fluorescent YFP-MutL, time-lapse microscopy and a mother-machine device in *E. coli*. The inspected text provides a concrete example of distinguishing short-lived repair-associated signals from longer-lived signals and checking the inferred aggregate mutation rate against mutation accumulation plus whole-genome sequencing. It is a useful measurement-design precedent, not a selected SBW25 assay or a matched W/A/M control panel.

The [source ledger](SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json) gives exact representation fingerprints and 13 passage anchors. The following are source-specific limits with direct consequences for the worksheet:

| Inspected source fact | Consequence for feasibility review |
| --- | --- |
| Results state that errors escaping MMR detection also escape YFP-MutL visualization; Discussion says the method cannot precisely quantify MMR detection efficiency | A detected molecular signal is not an independently complete mutation-birth census. An absence of signal cannot certify an origin-negative lineage. |
| The 2-minute observation interval misses a substantial fraction of short-lived foci; the authors apply a lifetime-based correction | Ask for independently supported temporal sensitivity and interval-censoring treatment. Do not transfer a source correction, cadence or lifetime threshold into TMD by default. |
| The article compares aggregate rate estimates from long-lived foci and MA+WGS over a range of conditions | Aggregate agreement is evidence about those measurements; it is not paired molecular identity and origin truth for each focus, nor a W/A/M classification map. |
| The mother machine carries expelled cells away; long-term analysis is restricted to mother cells | Tracked division exposure and retained-lineage fate do not identify molecular events in all lost daughters. The visible subpopulation must be part of the declared measurement frame. |
| Medium, induction, tags and microscopy are specified, and short-term bleaching/promoter controls are reported | Apparatus and reporter effects require matched assessment. Flowing microchannels are not automatically transportable to a structured pellicle with resident competition. |
| The authors report heterogeneous repair efficiency and non-Poissonian long-lived-focus inter-arrival times in their setting | A constant-rate event model requires independent justification for the target system. This result does not automatically choose a different count law for TMD. |

The article's lifetime threshold is an empirical classification convention for its strains and conditions. It is not a universal boundary between a repaired error and a route-changing mutation. No inspected evidence links every focus to a subsequent molecular allele, W/A/M route and complete lineage fate, and no independent native-origin truth is imported from this source. Source Methods describe separate population mutation-accumulation/sequencing experiments; Fig. 3 also identifies the wild-type MA+WGS rate as reused from Lee et al. (2012), rather than an additional newly collected 2024 cohort. Lack of a significant difference between aggregate rates is not an equivalence guarantee or an event-level sensitivity estimate.

Only the provider's article text representation was acquired. Its 87.5 MB source-data ZIP, supplementary notes, movies, reads and code were not acquired or replayed. The representation's checksum authenticates the stored representation, not a PDF or an original raw experiment. The existing Brody pedigree limitations are reused from the pinned [R19 public assessment](../pedigree_contract/README.md), without a fresh raw-source replay: optical ancestry and surviving descendant genomes do not independently recover the genotypes of extinct or unrecovered cells.

## Preserve loss rather than explaining it away

The lost-lineage worksheet should separate: known introduced identity subsequently lost; observed division with an ungenotyped daughter; a molecularly supported event with some unobserved descendants; and an entirely unobserved birth. Only the first three have an observed record from which a specified denominator might be built. None licenses counting the fourth from surviving winners alone.

For each state, request the last observed time, reason for missingness, available ancestral and molecular evidence, and the strongest supportable conclusion. An unobserved genotype is not a reference genotype. A surviving sister may constrain inherited states under a justified pedigree rule, but cannot rule out a new event unique to an unsampled branch. Recovery of one carrier need not satisfy a native caller requiring several carriers, an informative reference branch and callable sites. Event inclusion is a joint property of the complete caller and its sampling frame.

If invisible event classes have no independently supported finite loss/inclusion bound, record that the proposed all-birth interpretation is unavailable. Restricting an analysis to observed detectable lineages may define a different defensible estimand, but it requires explicit separate review and must not be described as filling the original all-birth requirement. This packet does not make that change.

## When source outcomes cannot be held out

The source outcome-access ledger must record what was seen, by whom, when and at which unit/cluster level before the candidate, eligibility rules, contexts, feature mapping and comparator choices were fixed. Source outcomes already inspected or used to select this candidate cannot become untouched held-out outcomes by hiding columns, downloading a new copy, assigning a new random split or citing a later publication date. Aggregate endpoint summaries can also leak information used for design; the reviewer must assess the affected context and shared cohorts, not only the individual rows explicitly opened.

Reanalysis of those outcomes can support training, descriptive checks or clearly labelled retrospective validation. Confirmatory held-out evaluation requires independent unexposed outcome units with defensible access control and a prospective freeze, or a later independently collected block under the admitted design. A public dataset is not automatically disqualified solely because it exists publicly, but a claim of unexposed evaluation needs a credible dated access and decision history. Already-known Karita endpoint results provide no such untouched outcome frame for this round. Splitting colonies sharing a founder, culture, paired arm or relevant batch across training/evaluation does not restore independence. A data version or preprint/journal duplicate is not another biological replication.

A cryptographic hash proves byte identity, not when a researcher learned an outcome. The requested registration evidence therefore includes independent dated receipts, access controls and amendment history. All actual accession/hash fields remain NULL.

## External assessment scope and resource decision

The packet is ready for a qualified microbial geneticist/ecologist, an imaging or lineage-measurement specialist, and a statistician with experimental-design and measurement-error expertise to assess. A molecular bioinformatician is needed if native caller/episode inference is proposed. These are required capabilities, not appointed people. Reviewers should document conflicts and which evidence they assessed independently, resolve disagreements explicitly, and sign a claim-specific recommendation.

The requested external deliverable would consist of the completed 44-field worksheet, an accepted/refused/deferred gate table, a smallest useful missing-measurement plan, an itemized resource estimate, access/license limits and a statement of claims the proposed design cannot identify. No quotation, contact, registration, pilot or protocol has been commissioned. Internal AI cross-review can check this packet's consistency and source interpretation; it does not supply the qualified external judgment requested in the worksheet.

Resource planning must distinguish independent biological units from technical repeats and account for attrition, rare events, observer/reference adjudication, marker validation, instrument occupancy, data storage and independent analysis. The six resource items have quantity, cost, capacity, lead-time and partner fields left NULL. Review should first decide whether truth and eligible units are available. More capacity cannot solve structurally missing truth.
